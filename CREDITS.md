## ANSI artwork

The menu panels selected for Renegade++ are from the `Roy_EarlyANSI` collection supplied with the project materials. The artwork is credited to **Roy**; the pieces carry Roy's signature in the source collection. The selected files are:

- `MAINMENU.ANS` — main menu header.
- `MMSMENU.ANS` — message-area and message-board header.
- `FILEDIR.ANS` — file-area header.
- `CHATMENU.ANS` — live-chat header.

The original collection paths, byte counts, and SHA-256 values are recorded in [`assets/ansi/imported/manifest.json`](assets/ansi/imported/manifest.json). The artwork is presented as supplied, with its own ANSI color sequences and signature retained.

Other artwork in `assets/ansi/imported/` is provided for the built-in gallery. Artist attribution may be present in the artwork itself or in its collection path; this repository does not claim authorship of those pieces.

The bulletin, quote, and tagline files marked as demo content were written for this sample installation. They do not represent real caller activity or quotations attributed to outside authors.
